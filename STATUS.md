# STATUS

- COVER (Ariel & Nina, CGDD 2026): a first-person stealth game about hiding a phone at Clearview Academy, a school where "what is seen, is safe".
- Story: the phone has been passed down through the same seat, Seat 31, since 1979. Six chapters run Monday to Friday, an homage to the Third Wave experiment (1967): rules, community, action, and a "day five" whose screen shows nothing.
- Stage: v2. Title page (New Game / Continue / Chapters, English or 中文, story speed 1x / 2x). Six chapters (about 40 min), an end page after each one, two endings, and a basement epilogue.
  - Ch.1 The Lesson (Monday): roll call, the phone in the desk, codes from the lecture, the chalk triangle.
  - Ch.2 Lunch (Monday): a camera and a Lunch Monitor, find the girl who hums, the staff door, the stairs.
  - Ch.3 Lights Out (Monday night): pretend to sleep while the warden checks, Contacts, flash the dorm across the yard.
  - Ch.4 Picture Day (Wednesday): prefects, the CAMERA app, a class-photo countdown to a moving X.
  - Ch.5 The Archive (Thursday): silent mode that fails at random, a noise meter, SEARCH every word, the old record, pigeons.
  - Ch.6 Inspection Day (Friday): get the phone back, Voss inspects the desks, hide it again or send it to everyone.
- Verbs: watch, peek, act, hide. Chapters add listen, pretend to sleep, photograph, smile, move quietly, silence the phone, read and search, and decide.
- Win: each chapter's goal (contact the triangle student / deliver the message and reach the stairs / signal the dorm / photograph 3 things and stand in the final photo / read the record and search all 8 words / choose an ending).
- Loss: three warnings (never shown as a number). The watcher takes the phone; retry from the start of the chapter.
- World: readable posters, some of which change when you look (smile, glitch, lunch, mirror, red X, Voss). The courtyard with the founder's oak and pigeons. Trees and buildings outside every window. All art is built in code; all sound is generated in code.
- Flowchart and tunables table are written to `docs/` and `site/data/` by `CGDD/Export Trace Docs`.
- `DEVLOG.md` logs only the game itself.
- The student site defaults to English. The nav can switch to Chinese. Chrome strings live in `site/lang.js`. Exported docs are English; Chinese is a display translation.
